How this sky was reconstructed

Location: central Kingston, 17.971° N, 76.793° W, sea-level approximation. Times use Jamaica’s UTC−5 offset; 8 PM local is November 16, 2005, 01:00 UTC. Positions are calculated at 15-minute intervals with precession and standard atmospheric refraction. Stellar proper motion is not applied; this is an illustrative sky chart rather than precision astrometry.
